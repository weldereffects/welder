Welder 0.1.2: a fix for drawing roto shapes.

## Fixed

- **Roto: clicks that didn't start a shape.** Holding a tool button a little too long (over a third of
  a second) opened the button's tool menu instead of choosing the tool, and while that menu was open
  the next click in the viewer only closed it. Now a click, even a slow one, chooses the tool (the
  menu needs a deliberate hold of over half a second, on the button), and a click in the viewer while
  a tool menu is open closes it and counts in the viewer too.
