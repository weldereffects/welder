Welder 0.2.7: deep compositing finished (GPU flatten and merge, deep ZDefocus, a deep checkbox on
Write), much cleaner TrackMask mattes, a playback cache that keeps its frames, and a fuller viewer
toolbar.

## New

- **Deep compositing, part 4.**
  - Flattening and DeepMerge's sample combining run on the GPU, with the CPU as the fallback.
  - ZDefocus fed a deep image defocuses each sample at its own depth, so what's behind an
    out-of-focus edge shows through its blur with no dark halo. A new **use deep samples** knob is
    on by default.
  - Rest the cursor on a deep image in the viewer to list that pixel's samples, front to back.
  (#134)
- **Write deep EXRs from Write.** A new **deep** checkbox in Write's EXR options writes deep
  scanline EXRs. It replaces the DeepWrite node from 0.2.6. Comps and scripts that use DeepWrite
  still work and get a Write with deep on. (#136)
- **Viewer toolbar.** (#126, #138)
  - An **auto proxy** toggle.
  - A **proxy** menu.
  - **ROI**: drag a box and only that area renders.
  - A **pause** button (P).
  - A **zoom** menu.

## New: AI

- **TrackMask mattes.** Mattes are solid inside with sharp edges at the plate's full size, instead
  of soft 18 px edges on a 4.6K plate. Patches where SAM was unsure are cleaned up (a new
  **min confidence** knob). A new **SAM High-Res** Mask Output re-segments the edge in tiles at
  the plate's own resolution, with no extra download. The viewer also no longer runs mattes through
  the view transform, so a solid matte shows as 1.0, not grey. (#135)

## Fixed

- **The playback cache keeps its frames.** Zooming out in Full res, changing the frame range or the
  viewer transform, undoing and turning off a wipe no longer re-render the cached frames. (#137,
  #138)
- Cryptomatte lists the layers your render actually has under their own names, such as Redshift's
  `cryptoMesh`. (#120, #138)
- Textured spheres and cylinders no longer show a dark seam where the texture wraps. (#125, #138)
- ScanlineRender's deep output is shaded the same way as its flat render. (#134)
