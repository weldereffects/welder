Welder 0.2.4: TrackMask grows into a full AI matte tool, OFlow and FlowBreath get much faster, and
the viewer stops stalling during playback. Also includes six issue fixes.

## New: AI

- **TrackMask: up to 16 objects.** Each object has its own anchors on any frame, correction
  keyframes, visibility keys and output channel. An edit re-tracks only the frames it changed.
  **Find text objects** makes objects from a text prompt, and **Re-find** picks up objects that
  were lost. A floating object manager over the viewer handles picking, renaming, routing and
  keyframes. Older saves load as one object. (#71, #73)
- **TrackMask: cleaner edges and more outputs.**
  - Mask Output can be RAW, Hard, Guided Smooth (the new default), ViTMatte or MEMatte. The
    ViTMatte and MEMatte refiners need their weights installed by hand.
  - A 4-channel mask combiner works on the new mask input.
  - Post passes: grow/shrink, feather, levels and temporal edge smoothing.
  - **Convert to Splines** turns the tracked mattes into animated PowerRoto or Roto shapes.
  - **Bake Masks to Disk** writes EXRs, and later bakes write again only the frames that changed.
  (#73)

## New

- **Cards from picked points.** In Scene3D, pick 3 or more points, then press C and click (or use
  **Card on selected points**) to fit a card to them. Drags that cross the horizon no longer fail.
  (#75)
- **Reset knobs to default.** Double-click a knob's name to reset it, or right-click a node's
  header and choose **Reset all knobs to default**. (#76, #89)
- **VectorGenerator directions.** A new `directions` knob. On `auto`, it solves only the backward
  vectors when FlowBreath is the only node reading them. (#88)

## Faster

- **OFlow.** Retiming a 4.6K plate at 25% speed went from about 6 s to 2.4 s a frame on a 4-core
  CPU, and the viewer's wait per frame from about 20 s to 7.5 s. OFlow also runs on the GPU now,
  with the CPU as the fallback. (#84, #90)
- **FlowBreath chains.** VectorGenerator time roughly halves when it feeds FlowBreath. (#88)
- **PointsToMesh.** A 20k-point mesh builds in 0.04 s instead of 1.9 s. Stray points, step edges
  and floors seen at a low angle come out cleaner. (#75)
- **Viewer.** Drags render at a lighter resolution while the mouse is held, then at full quality
  on release. The playback cache stores half floats, so it holds twice as many frames. (#74)

## Fixed

- Playback no longer gets stuck on one frame in flow comps (VectorGenerator to FlowBreath) while you
  edit knobs. (#74)
- With several viewers, each keeps its own f/stop, gamma, channel and matte settings. (#79)
- Cryptomatte finds Arnold layers such as `crypto_object00`. (#82)
- ScreenKeyer no longer keys out bright HDR highlights. A new **HDR highlights** option is on by
  default. (#80)
- ZDefocus no longer leaves black grain. (#86)
- Reconstruct and Depth no longer hang on "waiting for the worker". A stuck worker is now restarted.
  (#87)
